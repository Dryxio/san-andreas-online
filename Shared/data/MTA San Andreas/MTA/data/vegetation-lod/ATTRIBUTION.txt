Models and textures: Junior_Djjr / MixMods.com.br (2021).
Original installation instructions: Original-Readme.txt.
Redistribution permission for inclusion in Neon was confirmed by the project
owner during integration. This notice does not relicense the assets under GPL.

Neon uses verified stock-model correspondences, without installing the mod's
replacement IPL files or allocating its hard-coded model IDs. Missing or
unmatched definitions are listed in manifest.json and retain GTA rendering.

Neon adaptation: day/night vertex RGB is projected from the corresponding
stock GTA model onto the simplified mesh. Original geometry, UVs, textures
and vertex alpha are preserved. manifest.json records original pack hashes,
calibrated hashes and the donor model hash for reproducible regeneration.
